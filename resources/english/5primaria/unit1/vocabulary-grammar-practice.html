B. Present simple or present continuous?

Example

He plays hockey on Saturdays.

Rutina → present simple.

He is playing hockey now.

Ahora → present continuous.

Pistas del material: on Saturdays, every week, on Fridays → rutina. now, today, this afternoon → acción actual.
